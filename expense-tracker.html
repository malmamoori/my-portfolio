Expense Tracker

A desktop application for managing expenses, tracking income, and viewing monthly and yearly financial summaries.

About This Project

Expense Tracker is a desktop application built with Python, Tkinter and SQLite.

The application allows users to add, update and delete expenses, organize them by category, filter records by date, and track monthly and yearly spending.

It also includes monthly income tracking and automatically calculates the remaining balance after expenses.

Key Features

💰 Expense Management

Add, update and delete expense records easily.

🔍 Search & Filters

Search expenses and filter them by month, year and category.

📊 Financial Summary

Automatically calculates monthly and yearly expenses.

💵 Income Tracking

Set monthly income and calculate the remaining balance.
